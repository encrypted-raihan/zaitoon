Our Story

A Taste of
Arabia
In Kerala

Nestled opposite the Reliance Petrol Pump in Chandanathope, Zaitoon was born from a single obsession — to bring the slow-fire soul of Yemeni cooking to the heart of Kollam.

Every grain of rice in our Mandhi is seasoned with patience. Every cut of chicken in our Al Faham is marinated overnight. We don't rush flavour — we build it, layer by layer, the way it has been done across Arabia for centuries.

From humble porotta to grand Mutton Mandhi platters, Zaitoon is where Malabar hospitality meets the warmth of Middle Eastern tradition.
